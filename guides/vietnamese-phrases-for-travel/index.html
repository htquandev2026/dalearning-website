Vietnamese phrases for travel: eat, ride, pay

A few phrases make Vietnam far easier and friendlier: food stalls, motorbike rides and markets are where you will use them every day.

Polite and simple

  • “Cho tôi…” (Give me…) is how you order almost anything.
  • “Bao nhiêu tiền?” asks the price.
  • “Cảm ơn” is thank you; “anh” or “chị” in front of a sentence makes it warmer when speaking to someone a bit older.
  • Bowl is “bát” in the North and “tô” in the South. More regional words are in Northern vs Southern Vietnamese.

Ordering phở

Phở chín.Pho with well-done beef.
Phở tái nạm.Rare beef and flank.
Phở gầu.Pho with fatty brisket.
Cho thêm một quả trứng chần.Add a poached egg, please.
Bát to hay bát nhỏ?Large or small bowl?
Cho thêm quẩy.Add some fried dough sticks.
Ít bánh, nhiều thịt.Fewer noodles, more meat.
Cho xin ít chanh ớt.Some lime and chilli, please.
Nước dùng ngọt quá.The broth is so rich.

Street food

VietnameseEnglish
Cho tôi một cái bánh mì.One bánh mì, please.
Bánh mì thịt bao nhiêu tiền?How much is a pork bánh mì?
Cho thêm ớt nhé.Add some chilli, please.
Không cho rau mùi nhé.No coriander, please.
Ở đâu có bún chả ngon?Where’s good bún chả?
Ăn ở đây hay mang về?Eat here or take away?
Tôi muốn thử món này.I want to try this dish.
Món này ăn thế nào?How do you eat this?
Tôi ngồi đây được không?Can I sit here?
Cho tôi một đĩa cơm tấm.One plate of broken rice, please.

Taking a ride-app motorbike

Anh đến chưa?Are you here yet?
