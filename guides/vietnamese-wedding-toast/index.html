A Vietnamese wedding toast you can actually give

If you are marrying into a Vietnamese family, you may be asked to say a few words at the wedding or at the engagement ceremony. A short speech in Vietnamese, even read from a card, is one of the most remembered moments of the day.

The shape of a good toast

  • Ask permission to speak.
  • Greet both families respectfully: “Kính thưa hai bên gia đình.”
  • Thank the parents for welcoming you.
  • Apologise, lightly, for your Vietnamese. Everyone will love it.
  • Wish health to the elders and happiness to everyone.
  • Raise your glass.

Your toast, line by line

VietnameseEnglish
Con xin phép nói vài lời.Allow me to say a few words.
Kính thưa hai bên gia đình.Respected members of both families.
Con rất xúc động hôm nay.I’m very moved today.
Con cảm ơn bố mẹ đã đón nhận con.Thank you for welcoming me.
Tiếng Việt của con chưa giỏi, mong mọi người thông cảm.My Vietnamese isn’t great — please bear with me.
Con xin chúc ông bà mạnh khoẻ.I wish grandpa and grandma good health.
Chúc cả nhà hạnh phúc.Happiness to the whole family.
Con xin mời mọi người nâng ly.Please raise your glasses.
Vì sức khoẻ của bố mẹ!To your health, Mom and Dad!
Một, hai, ba, dô!One, two, three, cheers!
Con nói chưa hay, xin lỗi mọi người.Sorry if that wasn’t well said.
Con cảm ơn cả nhà đã lắng nghe.Thank you all for listening.

Tips

Keep it under a minute. Practise the greeting and the final cheer the most; that is what people hear clearly in a noisy room. “Một, hai, ba, dô!” (One, two, three, cheers!) is the cue for everyone to drink.
