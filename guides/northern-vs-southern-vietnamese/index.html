Northern vs Southern Vietnamese: which accent should you learn?

Vietnamese speakers from Hà Nội and from Saigon understand each other without trouble, but the accents sound different and some everyday words change. If you are learning to talk to a family, the right accent is simply the one they speak.

What changes

  • Tones: the North keeps six distinct tones; the South merges hỏi and ngã.
  • Sounds: the North says “d”, “gi” and “r” like a “z”; the South says “d” and “gi” closer to an English “y”.
  • Words: bố/ba (dad), không/hông (no), quả/trái (fruit), lợn/heo (pig).
  • Politeness: “vâng” is a Northern yes; “dạ” is used everywhere, and is the usual polite yes in the South.

How to choose

  • Family from the North, or living in Hà Nội: learn Northern.
  • Family from the South, or in Saigon, or in most overseas communities in the US: Southern is a common choice.
  • Not sure? Ask: “Nhà mình nói giọng miền nào ạ?”
  • You can switch later. The grammar and spelling are the same.
