Privacy policy

Dạ: Learn Vietnamese · Effective 21 November 2026

The short version

Dạ does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your learning progress, streak, stickers, settings and the name you type during set-up.
  • Voice recordings you make to practise speaking, and any family voice clips you add. These are saved inside the app on your device only and are never uploaded.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We cannot see it or recover it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only so you can record yourself and compare with the example audio. Recordings never leave your device.

Purchases

Purchases and optional tips are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, email or card details. The store only tells the app that a purchase was completed.

Children

Kids mode is designed for children and collects nothing. Purchases and settings sit behind a grown-up check. We do not show ads or use third-party analytics in any mode.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
