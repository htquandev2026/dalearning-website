Dạ

About Dạ

Dạ: Learn Vietnamese is an app for people who want to speak Vietnamese with family: children growing up abroad who want to talk to their grandparents, partners and in-laws of Vietnamese families, and anyone living, working or travelling in Vietnam.

The name comes from “dạ”, the polite way Vietnamese people answer an elder. It is often the first word a child says to bà and ông, and the first word a new partner says to the family.

What we believe

  • Family Vietnamese is different from textbook Vietnamese: the right word for each relative matters more than grammar tables.
  • Learners should hear the accent their family speaks, Northern or Southern.
  • A learning app for families should not track anyone. Dạ has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
